Logging in works again. Hacker News began rejecting embedded browsers, which is
how Glider used to sign you in, so login is now a form inside the app. Your
password is sent only to news.ycombinator.com and is never stored.

Also in this release:
- Rebuilt on Flutter 3.47 and updated for Android 16
- The display refresh rate is left to your device instead of being forced
- Links in comments show readable URLs again instead of percent-encoded ones
- Opening a Hacker News link no longer risks crashing the app
- An expired session shows an error instead of crashing
